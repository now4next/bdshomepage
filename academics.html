Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Philosophy

Mission

Developing Inquisitive Global Leaders

Beyond Dream Global Leader Scholars aims to develop inquisitive and conscientious students that will strive to become global leaders and life-long learners — optimistic in all situations, contributing meaningful work to their local, national, and global communities.

Vision

Imagining Life Beyond the Dream

Beyond Dream is the idea that students will imagine a life after achieving their dreams. Through altruism and perseverance, BDS fosters a passion for learning through active participation in diverse activities — with the support of a caring and dedicated community that empowers students to become creators of their own future.

Philosophy

Learning Through Personal Growth

BDS believes that learning must take place in a fun and eco-friendly environment. Finding one's dream comes from a focus on personal growth rather than ruthless competition and test scores. In a safe and supportive space, students develop a love of school and become lifelong learners — which ultimately leads to better academic success.

Mathematics: Algebra to Calculus

A rigorous, sequential pathway from foundational algebra through advanced calculus. Grades 9–12 follow an American standard sequence aligned with AP preparation.

  • • Algebra I Foundations of linear equations, functions, and systems
  • • Algebra II Advanced polynomials, exponentials, and complex numbers
  • • Pre-Calculus Trigonometry, sequences, and limits
  • • AP Calculus Differential and integral calculus for university-ready students

Beyond Academics

Student Life & Enrichment

Learning extends far beyond the classroom. Through clubs, projects, and structured study time, BDS students develop leadership, creativity, and independent learning habits.

Student Clubs

Student-led organizations that foster community, passion, and peer leadership. Members choose clubs based on interests and commit to their growth and contribution.

  • → Bookworm — Literary discussion & reading club
  • → Sports Club — Soccer, volleyball, athletics
  • → Band — Orchestra, ensemble, performance
  • → Yearbook — Student publication & storytelling

Signature Projects

Student-centered, real-world problem-solving that connects learning to community impact and personal discovery.

  • → Design Thinking — Innovation workshops & prototyping
  • → Self-Directed Learning — Habit formation & goal setting
  • → Community Problem-Solving — Chungju local impact projects
